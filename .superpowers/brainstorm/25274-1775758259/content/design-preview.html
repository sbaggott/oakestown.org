About

Kaya Oakes

Kaya Oakes is an author of six books. Her essays and journalism have appeared in The New Republic, Slate, Foreign Policy, The Guardian, The Washington Post, Sojourners, National Catholic Reporter, and more.

She co-founded the arts and culture magazine Kitchen Sink and serves on the editorial board of Killing the Buddha. She has taught at UC Berkeley's College Writing Programs since 1999 and was born and raised in Oakland, California, where she currently resides.
